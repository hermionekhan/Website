3D Modelling

With an obsession of futurism and a love for cyber-retro aesthetics, my 3D modelling emulates this through concepts from various real-life and conceptual influences. I mainly use Blender for modelling, texturing and rendering, with the adobe suite for post-production. I am currently learning external rendering software.


Graphic Design

Similarly to my 3D modelling style, I enjoy reflecting themes of futurism and collage in my graphic design. I tend to go for a cinematic output rather than something bright and joyful and particularly like applying my designs to mockups intended for a product purpose.
