Phantom Maternal (2024)

An interactive, hybrid novel which utilises the power of fictive text and 3D spaces in sequence to bring a short story to life.

Built within Unity Game Engine, Phantom Maternal uses 5 chapters: 1, 3 & 5 in written text and 2 & 4 as interactive 3D environments. This approach was used with intention to diversify the audience's experience and supplement the story with a multimedia approach.

The story flows linearly, the 3D environments giving the audience both spacial context and atmosphere while still enabling them to use their imagination. The use of both traditional and modern storytelling aims to heighten the audience's immersion and introduce demographics to a form of media they have possibly never entertained. This project ambitiously aims to bridge the gap between video game and novel in a literal approach, realised in a single digital executable.

Blurb

George is a responsible young boy at the mere age of ten, but he didn't anticipate having to travel cross-country solo for the first time today. It was an accident of course! He could barely make out which carriage his mother was in before the train started to roll away from the platform. He knew that travelling for the first time by himself is bound to be daunting, but is it supposed to be this creepy? The small station, the crackling speakers, the frail passengers, not to mention his new stoic, red-haired caretaker. And what about this train? It's glorious shining metal facade now seems like an illusion from the interior of cracked wallpaper and institutionalsied decor. But what's this? Something changed, something… obvious. Was the train always going this fast? Were the chairs always made of dark garish wood? And the walls… the walls are different. And over there was something in the field just now… it felt dark and intruding, garish and anticipanting. George didn't like this one bit. And where on earth is his mother?
